About Aqui Comes The Gato

Gato is lost and she needs your help to find her way back home. Gato fell asleep in a box of yarn and she woke up the next morning in the streets of the Gulf. How will Gato speak to the local cats? She can’t even speak Arabic. Join Gato on this road of discovery where she will decipher a completely new language on her journey home.

How It Works

  1. Explore Every Street

    Every street has a secret. Every sign, a clue. Can you read what the city is trying to tell Gato?

  2. Collect the Words

    Every word is important, how do you know if it's not?

  3. Piece Together the Language

    Match the words you've collected to what you see. Test your guesses on the local cats, since their replies will tell you if you got it right.
